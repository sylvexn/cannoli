Pointer-tracked 3D tilt + specular

JS sets --mx / --my from the pointer; CSS does the rest with perspective() rotateX/rotateY and a radial highlight at the cursor. ~15 lines of JS, the gem feel.

Animated conic sheen

A registered @property --gem-sheen angle makes a conic-gradient animatable — it sweeps around the facet on hover via mix-blend-mode:screen. Idle gems stay static = cheap.

Hue-matched dark glow

Stacked box-shadow layers colored from each gem's own hue, blooming against the #08080d surface. Layered grain adds the natural, non-digital texture.
